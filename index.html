The idea

For children who read differently.

Children with dyslexia may need more time, clearer spacing, audio and repeated practice. The goal is not to make the task easier — it is to make the learning path clearer.

Accessibility tools

Small controls. Big difference.

The website includes settings that help learners feel more comfortable while reading and practicing.

Aa

Dyslexia reading mode

Increases letter spacing and line height, keeps a calm background and uses a highly readable font.

◐

High contrast mode

Helps learners who need stronger separation between text, cards and background.

▣

Reduced motion

Stops animations for children who find moving elements distracting.

🔊

Text to speech

Lets students hear a word or instruction before answering.
